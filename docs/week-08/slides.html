6 / 14

Reliably wrong about

  • Structures that cannot fold flat
  • Flaps and tabs with nowhere to go
  • Substrates that don't exist at that thickness
  • Finishes that can't go on that material
  • Claims and marks that aren't real
  • Artwork flowing across folds that would break it

Two ways to fail

Lab — generate, then break

  1. Six prompts, structure varied
  2. Save every prompt with its output, as you go
  3. Pick the three most convincing
  4. Break each one — find what could not be made
  5. Partner confirms your failure from the image
  6. Attribution: prompt or tool?
  7. Select your direction
