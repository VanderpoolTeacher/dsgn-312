Week 8 Lab — Generate, then break

DSGN 312 · Packaging Design · in the room

About 60 minutes across two sessions. Not graded. Produces Phase 3.

What you need

  • A working generative tool account — checked in week 7
  • The AI log, open before you start generating
  • Your week 7 brief and specification
  • A partner

Steps

1 · Open the log first. Not after. Prompts cannot be reconstructed, and a log rebuilt from memory is fabricated evidence.

2 · Run at least six prompts against your own product, brand and audience. Vary the structure deliberately, not just the style — a tuck box, a sleeve over a tray, a tray and lid.

3 · Save every prompt with its output as you go. Paste the prompt, save the image, number them.

4 · Pick the three most convincing — the ones you would show somebody.

5 · Break each of the three. Find one specific thing that could not be made:

  • A flap with no slot
  • A finish that cannot go on that substrate
  • A fold that would tear across the grain
  • A structure that could not be cut from one sheet
  • A mark or claim that is not real

6 · Partner check — hand your failure and the image to a partner. Could this actually not be made?

A failure a partner cannot confirm is an impression. This is the standard the assignment is marked against, and it is cheaper to find out here.

7 · Attribution, per confirmed failure. Did your prompt cause it — you never named a structure — or would a precise prompt have failed the same way?

8 · Select your direction and write one sentence on what the generated work contributed.

That sentence may honestly be "nothing but a rejected option." That is a legitimate result and it scores fine.

The four questions

From the demo. Ask these of every output you are tempted by:

  1. What is it made of?
  2. How does it open?
  3. Where does the glue tab go?
  4. Could this be cut from one sheet?

If the image cannot answer, that is the finding.

Prompting for structure — a working shape

[FORM] for a [DIMENSIONS] [PRODUCT],
[SUBSTRATE] at [WEIGHT],
[CLOSURE],
[WHAT IS PRINTED ON WHICH PANEL]

Filled in:

Straight tuck box for a 90x60x25 mm soap bar,
350 gsm uncoated kraft,
tuck flap top and bottom with one glue tab,
product name on the front panel, ingredients on the back

Vary one element at a time. Changing three things gives you a different image and no information.

Before you leave

  • Six prompts and six outputs, logged as you went
  • Three broken, each with a failure a partner confirmed
  • An attribution for each
  • A selected direction, and one honest sentence on what AI contributed
