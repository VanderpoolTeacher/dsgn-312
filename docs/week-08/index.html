Week 8 · AI as a design tool, and where it fails

The only week in this course that teaches LO 6, and it has two sessions instead of three because Fall Break closes Friday.

You will generate more packaging concepts than you could draw by hand, and then you will learn to distrust them — specifically, precisely, and in a way somebody else can check.

Course outcomes: LO 6.

By the end of this week you can

  • Write a prompt that specifies structure, not only style.
  • Find a specific, checkable failure in a generated concept.
  • Say whether the prompt caused the failure or the tool did.
