Week 8 Assignment — Phase 3, concepts and the AI log

DSGN 312 · Packaging Design

Formative · satisfactory/unsatisfactory · required. Phase 3 of the final project — this becomes Component F.

Competency 6, Competency Rubric.

Component F is the only evidence for LO 6 in this course. There is no second assessment and no other week that touches it.

What to hand in

  1. Six prompts, verbatim, each with its output
  2. Three broken, each with a specific checkable failure and who confirmed it
  3. An attribution per failure — prompt or tool
  4. Your selected direction, and one sentence on what the generated work contributed
  5. Structural sketches of the direction you chose

You're done when

You have named a failure another person confirmed from the image.

Satisfactory

  • Prompts saved verbatim, with outputs, dated as produced
  • At least one specific, checkable failure — "a tuck flap with no slot," not "it didn't understand structure"
  • What you did instead
  • Attribution stated

Not yet

  • Outputs with no prompts
  • "It helped me brainstorm"
  • "It was all useless"
  • A failure nobody could confirm from the image

Watch out for

Reconstructing prompts afterwards. The integrity risk in this assignment. A student who generated without logging will write plausible prompts later — that is fabricated evidence, and it is usually visible because reconstructed prompts are too clean.

The general evaluation. "It struggled with structure" sounds like criticism and cannot be checked. Name the flap, the finish, the fold.

Blanket dismissal. Declaring the outputs worthless is not evaluation. It is the same non-answer as declaring them brilliant, and it scores the same.

Confusing pretty with useful. The output you would show somebody and the output that taught you something are often different images. Say which is which.

Why this one matters disproportionately

LO 6 is carried by this component alone. Every other outcome in this course is evidenced two or three times — LO 6 is evidenced here and nowhere else.

There is no fallback. If this is thin, the outcome is thin, and there is no later week that repairs it.

Submission

The log, the outputs, and the sketches, before the next session.

Week 9 draws the direction you select here, so choose one you are prepared to build.
