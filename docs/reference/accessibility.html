Accessibility

This site holds the student materials for DSGN 312. It is measured against WCAG 2.2 Level AA on every build, and this page is generated from those measurements rather than written by hand.

Keyboard and assistive technology

  • The week Overview / To Do control is a real tab list. Arrow keys, Home and End move between tabs; only the selected tab takes a tab stop.
  • Selected state is exposed with aria-selected, and each tab is tied to its panel with aria-controls.
  • With JavaScript off, no panel is hidden — both render in full, so nothing is lost.
  • Every interactive element takes a visible focus outline.
  • Movement is limited to a one-pixel hover shift, and that is disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Text reflows to a single column and the page does not scroll sideways; zoom is not disabled.

Known limitations

  • 1 pages have no level-1 heading. Their source begins at a second-level heading. week-13/assignment.html
  • 5 pages carry more than one level-1 heading, the slide decks among them — each slide is marked as its own heading, so reference/glossary.html has 4. Only one slide is visible at a time, but all of them are in the page for a screen reader.

What is not covered

These checks are automated. They do not establish that the writing is clear, that video and audio carry captions or transcripts, or that the site works well with a particular screen reader. Automated testing finds a minority of real barriers. If something here does not work for you, that is worth reporting even if this page claims everything passes.

Reporting a barrier

Tell your instructor. You do not need to have registered accommodations, and you do not need to know which guideline is at issue — describing what you could not do is enough.

Accommodations themselves, and access to course materials such as readings, video and equipment, are arranged through your institution’s accessibility or disability services office.
