The Sustainable Packaging System — Rubric

Step 3b of the Course Build Process

Covers every component except the presentation, which is scored on its own presentation rubric.

Every Meets row below is the criterion from Assessment Evidence, stated as an observable standard. It is not a softened version of it.

A. Brand and product brief — 10%

Evidences: Container — evidences no outcome.

Level Descriptor
Exceeds The retail context and audience are specific enough that two later decisions are already forced by them, and the student says which.
Meets Product named and physically measured. Brand stated in three sentences. Retail context is a named situation, not a category. Audience is stated in terms that rule something out.
Approaching All four present, but at least one is a category rather than a situation — "retail," "young professionals" — and constrains nothing downstream.
Not yet A section is missing, or the product has no measured dimensions and cannot be drawn from.

B. Materials and structure specification — 15%

Evidences: LO 3

Level Descriptor
Exceeds Names the disposal stream, the thing that could disqualify it, and a change made to the construction to protect it.
Meets Substrate, weight or caliper, and end-of-life path named. One alternative rejected with what ruled it out. The structural consequence of the choice is stated. Reading order on the buyer-facing panel is decided.
Approaching A material is specified with real numbers, but nothing was rejected — so the choice is asserted rather than reasoned. Or "recyclable" is claimed with no stream named and no threat to it identified.
Not yet Material named by type only — "eco-friendly kraft" — with no weight, no caliper, no disposal route. A preference, not a specification.

C. Prototype set — dieline, mockup, visualization — 20%

Evidences: LO 2

Level Descriptor
Exceeds The mockup assembles first time from the submitted dieline, and the student can point to the allowance made for the board's own thickness.
Meets Three artifacts of one design. The mockup is cut from the submitted dieline and assembles without being trimmed, taped into shape, or corrected. Panel count, proportion and closure carry across dieline, mockup and 3D visualization.
Approaching The dieline is clean and correctly layered but the mockup needed tape or trimming to close — a glue tab on the wrong face, or a panel short by the thickness of its own board.
Not yet Fewer than three artifacts, or they describe different objects — a dieline for one structure and a render of another.

D. The built packaging system — 20%

Evidences: LO 1

Level Descriptor
Exceeds The stranger describes the brand's positioning with the logo covered and names the audience decision that produced it.
Meets Primary and secondary packaging built at full size. It closes, holds the product, opens without destroying itself, and survives handling. With the logo covered, someone outside the class can describe who it is for and what it costs. Visual decisions trace to the named audience.
Approaching The object is built and works, but with the logo covered the brand reads as generic — the identity was carried by the mark alone. Or the audience is named and no decision traces back to it.
Not yet Not built, or submitted as renders only. It fails to close, does not hold the product, or is destroyed by opening.

E. Critique performance — 10%

Evidences: LO 4

Level Descriptor
Exceeds Finds a fault in one lens that changes the reading in another — an accessibility failure that is also a market failure — and says which fix serves both.
Meets Delivered live on instructor-supplied packaging not seen beforehand. All four lenses — market relevance, ethics, accessibility, visual communication — each find something the others did not. Every fault named comes with what would have to change.
Approaching Fluent and accurate, but one or more lenses go unaddressed, or faults are named without a consequence — the near-miss is a good description that never becomes a judgment.
Not yet Describes what is there without evaluating it, or addresses one lens only.

No artifact. If the performance does not happen, this component cannot be scored. A make-up on a different unseen package exists — tell the instructor beforehand rather than afterwards.

F. AI concept log — 10%

Evidences: LO 6

Level Descriptor
Exceeds Distinguishes an output that missed because the prompt was underspecified from one that missed despite a precise prompt, and draws the right conclusion from each.
Meets Concepts kept with the prompts that produced them. At least one specific, checkable failure named — a structure that cannot fold flat, a substrate that does not exist at that thickness, a finish that cannot be applied to that material, a claim that could not legally appear on a label — followed by what the student did instead.
Approaching Prompts and outputs are complete and the reflection is present, but the evaluation is general — "it didn't understand structure" — with no single failure named that could be checked by somebody else.
Not yet Outputs with no prompts, or "it helped me brainstorm," or blanket dismissal. Both extremes evaluate nothing.
