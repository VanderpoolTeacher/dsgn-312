Week 14 Workshop — The case study

DSGN 312 · Packaging Design · in the room

About 40 minutes. Not graded. One session — this is most of it.

What you need

  • Three dated states, in one folder. Week 8, week 11, week 13
  • Two transition sentences, written before you arrived
  • Your photographs
  • A partner

Steps

1 · Put the three states on screen, side by side, dated.

If you do not have three, say so now. In the first five minutes there is time to look — an autosave, an export, a photograph from the bench, a version in your week 11 submission. After that there is not.

2 · Write the two transition sentences, each naming its cause.

  • Week 8 → week 11: what the paper check and the bench build changed
  • Week 11 → week 13: what the test record changed. Your strongest material

3 · Partner check — hand your partner ONLY the transitions. Not the images, not the explanation.

Ask them: what caused each change?

If they cannot say, the cause is not on the page. Two minutes, and it catches the failure this module exists to prevent.

4 · Write the weakest-point sentence. The weakest remaining part of your sustainability claim, named plainly, with what you would do about it given resources.

5 · Assemble in order: problem → outcome → route → what is still wrong.

6 · Check your photographs at projection size. Not on your phone. Anything illegible at the front of a room is illegible in your portfolio.

If you have only two states

Say so in step 1, immediately.

If a genuine intermediate exists in any form — an autosave, an export, a photograph taken at the bench in week 10, an image in a submission — it counts.

A state reconstructed today does not, and the dates will show it. Better to have two states honestly and say so than three where one is manufactured.

What a transition sentence looks like

❌ "Then I refined the structure and improved the closure."

✅ "The paper check showed the tuck flap meeting the opposite panel edge-to-edge with nothing to enter, so the flap gained 0.8 mm — two calipers — and the dieline was redrawn."

✅ "Two of three testers opened it from the base. The closure moved to the short panel and gained a thumb notch."

Cause, then change. In that order, because that is the order it happened.

Before you leave

  • Three dated states in one folder, on screen
  • Two transition sentences, each passing the partner check
  • The weakest-point sentence
  • A case study in the right order
  • Photographs that survive projection
