Week 10 · Building it

The week the project stops being a file. You cut your dieline in the board you specified and find out whether it closes.

Course outcomes: LO 1, LO 2.

> This week is observed, not submitted. Whether your mockup assembles from your submitted dieline without tape, trimming or correction is part of LO 2's evidence — and it is invisible afterwards, because a taped model photographs identically to a folded one. It is recorded at the bench or not at all.

By the end of this week you can

  • Cut, crease and assemble at full size in the specified material.
  • Test closure, protection and opening by handling the object.
  • Produce a 3D visualization that matches the object you built.

AssessmentFormative · satisfactory/unsatisfactory · observed at the bench.
