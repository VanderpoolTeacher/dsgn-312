Paper told you about geometry

Board tells you about everything else

  • It resists the fold
  • It has grain
  • It is thick — the allowance is finally load-bearing
  • It shows

3 / 14

Cut first. Crease second.

Creasing first makes the board curl. Every cut after that wanders.

4 / 14

A dull blade tears board

That ragged edge is not your drawing and not the material.

Change the blade. They're free.

5 / 14

Two or three light passes

Pushing harder makes the knife wander off the rule.

6 / 14

A crease is a compression

Bone folder. Metal rule. Firm, single pass.

A fingernail is not a crease.

7 / 14

Grain

Fold with it — clean. Against it — the surface cracks.

On a printed panel, the crack goes through your artwork.

8 / 14

The handling test — do all seven

  1. Close it
  2. Product in — does it fit?
  3. Open it
  4. Close it again — twice survivable?
  5. Set down, pick up
  6. Stand it as it would stand on a shelf
  7. Invert it

Last chance to find your own faults before week 11 finds them.

9 / 14

The visualization must match what you built

Same panels. Same proportions. Same closure.

A render that improves the object is a different design.
