Week 12 Assignment — Compliance audit of your own package

DSGN 312 · Packaging Design

Formative · satisfactory/unsatisfactory. Competency 4 applied to your own work.

The module summative this week was the live critique. It was assessed in the room and there is nothing to submit for it. This assignment is separate and lighter — it turns the week's teaching onto your own project, four weeks before you present it.

What to hand in

A compliance and accessibility audit of your own packaging system.

  1. Required information — what your category demands, what your package carries, and what is missing
  2. Where it sits — is anything on a panel that cannot be read, or across a fold?
  3. Accessibility, against the three groups:
    • Reading — contrast, size, colour-only distinctions, curved or folded surfaces
    • Opening — one-handed, limited grip, force required
    • Handling — weight, shape, tactile differentiation
  4. Your own claims — checkable or decorative? Name any that cannot be supported
  5. The fixes you are making, and any you are refusing with a reason

About a page.

You're done when

You have found at least one real failure in your own package and said what you are doing about it.

Everyone has one. A student who reports a clean audit has not audited — they have described their intentions.

Watch out for

Auditing what you meant. You know what the type says and where the opening is. Test it the way you tested in week 11 — cold, with the gloves if they help.

Missing the colour-only distinction. If your system has variants and colour is the only difference, that is a failure and it is invisible to you.

Claims you cannot support. Your week 7 specification named a stream and a disqualifying feature. Does your surface design claim more than the specification supports? This is the commonest ethics failure in student work and it is entirely self-inflicted.

Refusing everything. Some fixes are genuinely out of scope with three weeks left. Say which and why — that is legitimate. Refusing all of them is not an audit.

Why this is here

Week 15 asks you to volunteer the weakest point in your own sustainability claim before you are asked, and the rubric rewards it.

This audit is where you find it. Four weeks is enough to fix a contrast problem, move a panel, or drop an unsupportable claim. Three days is not.

Submission

One page, before the next session.
