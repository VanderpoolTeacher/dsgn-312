Contrast is always against something

Bold compared to what?

If everything in the row is bold, bold is the background.

Name the other term

Not an explanation An explanation
Eye-catching Only matte in a row of gloss — holds still while the others glare
Cleaner Four elements against eleven — legible at two metres
More premium The only one with no photograph, in a category where everyone photographs the food

11 / 14

Material is a visual variable

  • Matte holds still · gloss glares under a spotlight
  • Uncoated takes ink duller — quieter, warmer
  • Foil is contrast at distance, illegible up close
