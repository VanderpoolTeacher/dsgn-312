Week 13 Lab — The file clinic

DSGN 312 · Packaging Design · in the room

About 60 minutes. Not graded — produces the module summative.

Files are opened and inspected, not described.

What you need

  • Revised files, week 11's changes made
  • The physical mockup — the reference for every bleed check
  • Your product, and week 7's specification
  • Your destination, in one line
  • A partner

Steps

1 · Open the file with the instructor and step through the layers. You name each one and what it instructs.

2 · Check cut and crease separation. Anything on the wrong layer is fixed in the session, not noted for later.

3 · Check bleed at every fold, against the physical mockup. Every crease on the object corresponds to a line in the file — check them one by one.

4 · Check dimensions against week 7's specification and the product itself.

Drift is common after week 11's revisions — you changed a panel to fix a test failure and did not propagate it.

5 · Export to your destination's requirements.

6 · State, setting by setting, which requirement each one satisfies. Out loud.

"3 mm bleed because the trim drifts and artwork stopping at the edge leaves a white sliver on the fold."

A reason per setting. This is what the summative marks.

7 · Partner check — swap exported files and open each other's cold.

Anything your partner cannot interpret without you explaining is not a handoff.

8 · Confirm your finish is possible on your specified substrate.

Where it is not: change the finish, not the substrate. The substrate was reasoned in week 7 and carries LO 3. The finish was decoration.

The handoff checklist

Work through the checklist. It is the same list a printer would run.

Expect dimension drift

If your panel dimensions no longer match week 7, one of two things happened:

  • You revised deliberately in week 11 — good, and the specification should be updated to match
  • Something slipped — a nudge, a scale, a copy-paste

The product is on the desk. Measure it against the file.

Before you leave

  • Layers separated, DIELINE-REF excluded from the export
  • Bleed past every fold, checked against the object
  • An export matching a stated destination
  • A reason for every setting, said out loud
  • A partner who opened your files cold and understood them
  • A finish that can actually go on your board
