Week 13 Assignment — Phase 7, revised system and production files

DSGN 312 · Packaging Design

Module summative. Phase 7 — completes Component D and half of Component G.

Competencies 3 and 5, Competency Rubric.

What to hand in

  1. The revised packaging system — dieline, artwork, rebuilt mockup if the structure changed
  2. Production files, exported to a stated destination
  3. Your destination, in one line
  4. A settings table — every significant setting, and which requirement it answers
  5. The finish decision, including anything your substrate ruled out

You're done when

Every setting names the requirement it answers.

"Exported as PDF/X-1a" reports a choice.

"3 mm bleed because the trim drifts and artwork stopping at the edge leaves a white sliver on the fold" explains one.

A correct file with no reasoning behind it meets the technical standard and not the outcome.

Meets

  • Layers separated, DIELINE-REF excluded from the export
  • Bleed past every fold
  • Dimensions match the product and the specification
  • Export matches a stated destination
  • A reason per setting

Exceeds

You name a setting that had to change for this substrate specifically, and why the default would have failed on it — an uncoated profile, a heavier bleed for a bulkier board, a resolution decision driven by absorbency.

Not yet

  • No destination stated
  • A crease on the cut layer, or reference marks left in the export
  • Settings reported without reasons
  • A finish specified that cannot go on the substrate

Watch out for

Dimension drift after week 11. You changed a panel to fix a test failure and did not propagate it. The product is the reference.

The impossible finish. Spot UV on uncoated recyclable board. The substrate stays — it carries LO 3 and it is part of your material claim. Change the finish.

Reference layers in the export. Your notes will print.

Reporting instead of explaining. The whole difference between Meets and Not yet is a because.

Assuming you can keep revising. Week 14 has one session. Anything unresolved this week stays unresolved into the presentation.

Keep your dated states

You now have three:

  1. The concept selected in week 8
  2. The pre-revision state saved in week 11
  3. This week's production version

Week 14 assembles them, and week 14 has one session. Put them in one folder now, named and dated, and you have removed the single most common week 14 emergency.

Submission

Files, settings table, and the finish decision, before the next session.
