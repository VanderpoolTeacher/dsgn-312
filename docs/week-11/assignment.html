Week 11 Assignment — Test record and first revision

DSGN 312 · Packaging Design

Module summative. Competencies 1 and 5, Competency Rubric.

What to hand in

  1. Behaviour records from at least two strangers — what they did, in sequence, with times or counts
  2. Verbatim answers to the three questions, from both
  3. The covered-logo result
  4. Your sealed predictions, and where they were wrong
  5. The revision list — each item traced to a specific observed behaviour, marked dieline / artwork / refused with reason
  6. The revision you made, with the dated before-state alongside it

You're done when

At least one change traces to something a specific person did or said, and you can name what would have gone wrong if it had stayed.

Meets

  • Behaviour recorded, not opinion
  • Two testers minimum
  • One revision, traceable, made in the file
  • The dated pre-revision state kept

Exceeds

You name a result you chose not to act on, and give the reason — a constraint, a material claim, a competing requirement. Knowing which findings to refuse is a designer's judgment, not an omission.

Not yet

  • Opinions instead of behaviour
  • One tester
  • A revision with no antecedent — a preference
  • A change made to the photograph or the explanation rather than the file
  • No dated pre-revision state

Watch out for

Explaining the package. The failure that voids the test. If it happened, say so and report the fresh stranger's result.

Polite feedback recorded as data. "They said it was nice." You were meant to be watching, not listening.

One agreeing tester. If the first one confirmed your prediction, you have not tested anything yet.

Revising the presentation instead of the design. "I'll explain it on the day" is not a revision. The change goes in the dieline or the artwork.

Overwriting your file. Week 14 needs three dated states and this is the middle one. It cannot be reconstructed and no later week repairs it.

If the covered-logo test came back blank

Report it plainly. It means your week 7 audience statement stayed in the document.

This is a strong submission, not a weak one, if you identify which decision should have carried the audience and did not — and put it on the revision list. You have four weeks.

Why this is a summative

This creates the middle of the three dated states Component G requires.

Week 14 assembles them. If you revise nothing here, you arrive with two states and cannot evidence iteration — which is a third of Component G, and there is no way to manufacture it afterwards that the dates will not expose.

Submission

Records, list, revision, and the dated before-state, before the next session.
