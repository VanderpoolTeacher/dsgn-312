Week 11 · Putting it in front of people

You hand your package to somebody who did not make it and watch what happens. The module summative — and the week your week 7 audience statement gets tested.

Course outcomes: LO 1, LO 5.

Requires week 10's built mockup. Materially. There is no substitute.

By the end of this week you can

  • Run a test without explaining the package first.
  • Record where a tester hesitated, misread, or could not open it.
  • Convert a test result into a change in the file rather than in the photograph.

AssessmentModule summative. Competencies 1 and 5.
