Lab — the paper check

  1. Print at 100% — verify with a rule
  2. Cut and fold in paper
  3. Put the product in it
  4. Assemble fully, including tabs
  5. Fix in the file, not on the paper
  6. Reprint. Expect two rounds. Budget three.
  7. Partner assembles yours without instruction
  8. Check bleed at every fold
