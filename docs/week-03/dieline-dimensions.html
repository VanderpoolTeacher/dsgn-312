DSGN 312 · Week 3 lab

A straight tuck box. Same dimensions for the whole room, so that failures are comparable.

The box

Width 80 mm
Depth 40 mm
Height 120 mm
Board Supplied — approx. 0.4 mm thick

Print check dimension: the overall flat width of your drawing should measure 248 mm. Put a rule on it after printing. If it is not 248 mm, your print is scaled — reprint before cutting.

What to draw

  • Front, back, and two side panels
  • Tuck flaps top and bottom
  • Dust flaps on the sides of each tuck
  • One glue tab on a vertical edge

Layer names

Exactly these:

Layer Holds
CUT Every line the blade goes through
CREASE Every line the board folds along

Nothing else on either layer.
