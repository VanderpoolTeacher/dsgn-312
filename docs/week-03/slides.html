Every form is a trade

Form Buys Gives up
Straight tuck Cheap, flat-packs, re-closable Weak under compression
Sleeve Elegant, minimal material Protects nothing
Tray and lid Strong, feels expensive Two dielines, two costs
Clamshell Protects, visible, tamper-evident Often needs scissors

7 / 14

Four ways a box fails to close

  1. Glue tab on a face that is already covered
  2. Panel short by the thickness of its own board
  3. Crease missing, or made with a fingernail
  4. It printed at the wrong size
