Week 3 Lab — Draw it, print it, fold it

DSGN 312 · Packaging Design · at the bench

About 60 minutes across the two sessions. Not graded. What fails here becomes this week's assignment.

What you need

  • The dimensions sheet — everybody draws the same box
  • Design software with layer control
  • Card, three sheets. You will need at least two
  • Knife with a fresh blade, metal rule, bone folder, cutting mat

Steps

1 · Draw a straight tuck box to the dimensions on the sheet. Everybody the same — common dimensions make the room's failures comparable, which is the point of the round-up at the end.

2 · Cut and crease on separate named layers. Name them CUT and CREASE. Check before you print.

3 · Print at 100%.

4 · Verify the scale. Put a rule on the known dimension marked on your sheet. Ten seconds. If it is wrong, reprint — this is the only failure that teaches nothing.

5 · Cut. Fresh blade, two or three light passes.

6 · Crease with the bone folder against the rule, from the correct face for each fold.

7 · Fold and assemble. Follow the assembly order the structure requires.

8 · Try to close it.

9 · STOP. Do not repair anything.

Write down what failed first. Not everything that is wrong — the first thing. Everything after it is a consequence, and diagnosing a consequence as a cause is the mistake this step prevents.

10 · Second attempt. Change one thing only.

One. Students who change three things learn nothing about which mattered.

11 · Round the room — name your first failure.

Diagnosing, not guessing

Work through these in order before deciding:

Ask If yes
Did you verify the print scale? If not, do that first. Nothing else is diagnosable
Does the glue tab land on a face that is still available at that point in assembly? If not — tab on the wrong face
Are the end flaps the same as the box depth? If so — they meet exactly and there is nothing to tuck
Do the panels wrap around the board's own thickness? If not — the panel is short by its own caliper. You do not have this number yet. Week 4 gives it to you
Did you crease with a tool, from the correct face? If not — the crease, not the drawing

What to write down

For your first failure:

  • What you observed — what physically would not happen
  • Where it is in the drawing — the panel, the tab, the flap
  • Which of the four causes it is

❌ "It didn't work."

✅ "The end flaps are both 60 mm, the same as the box depth, so they meet edge to edge and there is nothing to tuck into."

Expect this to fail

Most of the room's first attempt will not close. Three sheets are provided because two attempts is normal.

If yours closes first time, check it properly — product in, closed, opened, set down. A box that closes but will not reopen has failed differently.

Before you leave

  • One folded box, however badly it went
  • The first failure, written, before you repaired anything
  • What you changed on the second attempt, and whether it worked
