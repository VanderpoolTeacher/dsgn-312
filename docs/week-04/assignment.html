Week 4 Assignment — The specification

DSGN 312 · Packaging Design

Module summative. Competency 3, Competency Rubric.

What to hand in

A specification somebody could order from.

1 · The product What it is, and its dimensions
2 · Current material As far as it can be determined
3 · Your substrate Name, grammage or caliper, supplier availability
4 · The disposal stream The named route, with conditions
5 · The disqualifying feature What in your construction most threatens that route
6 · The rejected alternative What it was, and what ruled it out
7 · Structural consequence What your caliper means for the dieline

One to two pages.

You're done when

Somebody could order your material from a supplier using only what you wrote — and could say why the other option lost.

Meets

  • Substrate named with grammage or caliper
  • Disposal stream named, with the disqualifying feature identified
  • One alternative rejected, with what ruled it out
  • The structural consequence stated

Exceeds

You name the stream, the disqualifying feature, and a change you made to the construction to protect it. Dropping the gloss lamination for a matt varnish, moving to a water-based adhesive, losing the window. That is the difference between knowing the threat and acting on it.

Approaching

A material specified with real numbers, but nothing rejected — the choice is asserted rather than reasoned. Or "recyclable" claimed with no stream and no threat named.

Not yet

Material named by type only — "eco-friendly kraft" — with no grammage, no caliper, no route. A preference, not a specification.

The rejected alternative carries the mark

This is the part students treat as padding and it is where most of the grade sits.

A single material named with no competitor is an assertion. The reasoning only exists where something lost. One sentence on what you considered and why it failed is worth more than three paragraphs on what you chose.

Watch out for

Unsourced numbers. Your partner flagged them in the lab. Fix them — do not resubmit a number you cannot account for.

"Sustainable" doing the work. The word is in the project title and in LO 3, where it describes an aim. As a claim about your specific package it is not evidence. Name the stream, name the threat.

Confusing grammage and caliper. They answer different questions and a dieline needs the second.

A disqualifying feature you did not look for. "There isn't one" is almost always "I did not check the lamination, the adhesive, or the closure."

Rejecting a straw man. "I rejected solid gold" is not a rejection. The alternative has to be one a reasonable person would have considered.

Why this is the module summative

Week 7 is this assignment again, on a product you chose — that is Component B of the final project, and the standard is set here.

The supplied product this week is deliberate. Students defend materials they have become attached to, and the attachment starts in week 7. This is the last time you get to reason about a material without wanting a particular answer.
