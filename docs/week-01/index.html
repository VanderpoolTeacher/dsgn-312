Week 1 · What a package is doing

The first week of the course, and the one that decides whether students think of packaging as a surface or as an object. Everything is built around a single physical act: opening something slowly enough to notice where it fought back.

Course outcomes: LO 1, LO 4 — both introduced, neither assessed here.

By the end of this week you can

  • Name what a package does besides contain the product — protect, inform, sell, and survive a supply chain.
  • Identify the moment a package was chosen on a shelf and say what won it.
  • Open a package and record the three points where it resisted.

AssessmentFormative · satisfactory/unsatisfactory · required.
