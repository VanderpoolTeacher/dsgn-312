Four jobs

  • Protect — it has to arrive intact
  • Inform — some of it is your decision, much of it is not
  • Sell — it has to be chosen, from a row, at distance
  • Survive — filling line to living room

5 / 15

They compete

Protect ↔︎ Sell A tamper seal protects and obstructs
Inform ↔︎ Sell Required text takes the space the brand wants
Survive ↔︎ Protect Thicker board protects, costs, ships heavy
Sell ↔︎ Survive Uncoated reads honest, marks with every fingerprint
