Week 2 Assignment — The brand audit

DSGN 312 · Packaging Design

Module summative. Assessed against Competency 1 of the Competency Rubric.

Terms follow the Glossary.

What to hand in

An audit document. Not bullet points — a document somebody could act on.

1 · The package What it is, photographed masked and unmasked
2 · What it claims In words and otherwise — material, structure, colour, typography, tone, and what is absent
3 · Your prediction Written before the test, reproduced as written
4 · What the stranger received Verbatim answers to all three questions
5 · The gap Where prediction and answer diverged
6 · The decision Which design decision produced the gap

Two to three pages, with photographs.

You're done when

Your audit identifies identity carried by something other than the mark.

That is the standard, and it is why the mark is covered. An audit that describes the logo has found the one element the test removes.

Meets, concretely

  • The claims section names at least three carriers other than the logo — structure, material, typography, tone, absence.
  • The verbatim answers are reproduced, not summarised.
  • The gap is named specifically, and the decision behind it is a decision somebody made about the object.

Exceeds

The stranger describes the brand's positioning and you name the audience decision that produced it — the choice, made by whoever designed this, that caused a person who had never seen it to say what they said.

Watch out for

Describing the logo. The commonest failure and the one the test is built to prevent. If your audit's best observation is about the mark, you have written about the thing that was covered.

Summarising the stranger. "They thought it was fairly expensive" is your compression of what they said. "Looks like it'd be twenty-odd quid, maybe more" is the evidence. Reproduce the words.

An audit that is an opinion. "The packaging is quite effective" is a view. The document is a comparison — prediction against answer against decision.

Three bullet points. You may have run a genuinely good test and still not produced an audit. This is a module summative and it is judged as a document.

Stopping at one stranger who agreed with you. Covered in the lab, and it is where confident students lose marks.

If your package turned out to be anonymous

Document the absence. What could this package have carried, and does not? Which of the five carriers is doing nothing here?

This is a harder audit to write and it is worth more, not less. Do not swap the package for an easier one.

Submission

One document with photographs, before the next session.

Keep the masking on until it has been marked — a claim about what was covered is checkable only while it is covered.
