What else carries identity

  • Structure — a silhouette you would know in the dark
  • Material and finish — uncoated reads honest, gloss reads clinical
  • Typography that is not the wordmark — the small print
  • Tone of copy — "Directions for use" vs "how to use me"
  • What is absent — no product shown, no face, no price

The test

  1. Mask every mark — front, back, base, seal, embossing
  2. Write the prediction
  3. What is it? Who is it for? What does it cost?
  4. Record word for word
  5. Compare
