Week 2 · Brand in three dimensions

The module summative, and the week that removes the logo. You will find out how much of a brand's identity is carried by the mark and how much by everything else — by covering the mark and asking somebody who does not know the brand.

Course outcomes: LO 1.

By the end of this week you can

  • Locate a brand's identity in decisions other than the logo.
  • Audit an existing package for what it claims and what it delivers.
  • Predict what a package communicates with the mark covered.
