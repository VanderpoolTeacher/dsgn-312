Week 2 · Shelf impact

Hierarchy under the conditions packaging actually faces — small, curved, badly lit, two metres away. You have organised a page before. A shelf is not a page.

Course outcomes: LO 1.

By the end of this week you can

  • Rank what is read first, second and third on a buyer-facing panel.
  • Explain why one package wins on a shelf in terms of contrast rather than taste.
  • Photograph a shelf and diagram the hierarchy actually operating on it.
